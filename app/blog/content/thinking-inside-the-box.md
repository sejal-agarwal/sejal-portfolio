Generative AI can help people build interfaces quickly, but an empty chat box asks users to know both what they want and how to describe it. My research explores a different approach: guiding people through focused questions that help turn an early idea into a concrete interface.

## Designing guided inputs

The project sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction and AI-assisted creation. We studied how structured prompts can reduce ambiguity while still leaving room for users to make meaningful design decisions.

This work was accepted to [Graphics Interface Conference ’26](https://graphicsinterface.org/). I’ll share more about the study, design process, and findings here as the work develops.
